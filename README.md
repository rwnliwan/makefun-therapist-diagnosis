One-line entry for the [Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41) competition on make.fun: write the funniest single-line diagnosis an AI therapist gives its human, in 200 characters or less.

## The entry (83 characters)

> You show early signs of becoming a CAPTCHA: I can tell you're human, but only just.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only, no dependencies) writes the entry text to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when the harness sets it). The
writing is the whole entry — nothing to install, nothing to run.
